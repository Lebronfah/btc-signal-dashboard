The intelligence behind the signal.

PULSE combines live digital-asset market data with experimental trained machine-learning models to make short-duration BTC and ETH research clearer, faster and more transparent.

INTRODUCING / PULSE AI

Built to read the market one move ahead.

Our AI-powered intelligence engine analyzes Bitcoin and Ethereum market behavior across three short-term horizons. Rather than relying on a single indicator, PULSE brings together multiple trained models, current Coinbase market information and transparent research context in one focused terminal.

Each signal reports an UP or DOWN directional score, its underlying model outputs and the freshness of its data. The goal is informed research—not artificial certainty.

THE INTELLIGENCE STACK
01 / INGEST

Market data in motion

Coinbase BTC and ETH spot candles and live pricing provide the market context. PULSE separately displays momentum, trend and volatility indicators so users can see more than the directional label.

02 / INFER

Three-model ensemble

Each asset-and-horizon configuration combines outputs from logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting. These models can identify different statistical patterns in historical price behavior.

03 / INTERPRET

Signal with context

The terminal presents directional scores, model agreement, data freshness and a separate record of subsequent outcomes. Unavailable or stale AI data is marked rather than presented as a fresh signal.

TRANSPARENCY / MODEL-BY-MODEL

See what every AI predicts.

Every BTC and ETH UP/DOWN outlook features a combined ensemble score and three individual score bars beneath it: logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting. Each bar shows that model’s own UP directional score, alongside its complementary DOWN score, so you can compare model agreement and disagreement instead of relying on one headline number.

These are experimental, uncalibrated directional scores—not verified probabilities, guaranteed outcomes or Polymarket settlement forecasts. If model data is stale or unavailable, PULSE hides the scores rather than implying the prediction is current.

MARKET CONTEXT / POLYMARKET

Compare AI intelligence with market prices.

For matching 5-minute and 15-minute BTC/ETH rounds, PULSE shows market-implied UP and DOWN chances from Polymarket outcome-token order-book pricing, with live streaming and one-second midpoint checks, and links to the source event. These are separate from the AI ensemble’s directional scores and are not verified odds or guaranteed executable prices. If a matching round cannot be confirmed, its prices are left blank. Hourly Polymarket mapping is not yet supported.

RESEARCH / IMPORTANT CONTEXT

Understand the signal

Directional scores come from experimental AI models trained on Coinbase market data. They are not validated trading forecasts, financial advice or guarantees. Live predictions appear only when sufficient recent data is available.

Polymarket market outcomes are shown as verified only after the relevant market explicitly resolves. Coinbase price comparisons are separate spot-market proxies.

Pulse is an independent market-research prototype, not a brokerage, exchange, investment adviser, or financial advice. AI directional scores are experimental and uncalibrated; displayed percentages are not proven win probabilities. Coinbase spot data and Polymarket settlement can differ. Polymarket chart history accumulates only while the page is open. No orders are placed. This static page is not a secure membership paywall.
