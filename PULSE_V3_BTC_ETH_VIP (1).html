OUR APPROACH / TRANSPARENCY

Technology-led. Evidence-first.

PULSE is designed around quantitative market analysis and an ambition to incorporate advanced AI research as those capabilities are developed and independently evaluated. The current dashboard uses transparent technical-indicator heuristics; it has not been demonstrated to use the world’s leading AI models or to produce reliably accurate predictions. We believe trust starts with describing the technology accurately.

Independent research only. No trades are executed on this platform. No forecast guarantees an outcome or constitutes investment advice.

RESEARCH / IMPORTANT CONTEXT

Understand the signal

Directional estimates are experimental, rule-based heuristics derived from Coinbase market data. They are not validated AI forecasts, financial advice or guarantees. Live predictions appear only when sufficient recent data is available.

Polymarket market outcomes are shown as verified only after the relevant market explicitly resolves. Coinbase price comparisons are separate spot-market proxies.

Pulse is an independent market-research prototype, not a brokerage, exchange, investment adviser, or financial advice. Signals are heuristic and uncalibrated; displayed percentages are not proven win probabilities. Coinbase spot data and Polymarket settlement can differ. No orders are placed.
